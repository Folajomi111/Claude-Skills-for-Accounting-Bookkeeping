
# Best Claude Skills for Accounting & Bookkeeping

Accounting and bookkeeping involve a lot of repetitive work.

From recording transactions and categorizing expenses to reconciling bank statements, preparing financial reports, and organizing tax records, there's always something that needs your attention.

And when you're managing the books for multiple clients or businesses, these tasks can quickly become overwhelming.

That's where Claude Skills can help.

I've put together the **Accounting & Bookkeeping Skills Bundle**, a collection of 14 Claude Skills designed to help accountants, bookkeepers, and small business owners handle everyday financial tasks more efficiently.

These skills help Claude follow structured accounting workflows instead of requiring you to explain the same process every time.

Some work with documents and spreadsheets you upload, while others may require access to accounting software, financial records, or external tools.

Here are the 14 skills included in the bundle and what each one can help you do.

---

## 1. Bookkeeping Setup Skill

Before you start recording transactions, you need a proper bookkeeping system.

That means choosing the right accounting method, organizing your accounts, and deciding how different financial activities should be recorded.

The **Bookkeeping Setup Skill** helps you establish this foundation.

For example, if you're setting up bookkeeping for a new small business, you can provide details about the business, its industry, and how it handles money.

Claude can then help you create a suitable accounting structure and organize the information needed to start keeping accurate records.

**What this skill can do:**

- Set up a chart of accounts.
- Organize income and expense categories.
- Help establish cash or accrual accounting workflows.
- Define transaction recording procedures.
- Create bookkeeping checklists and approval rules.

**Best for:** Bookkeepers setting up new clients, accountants, and small business owners starting their bookkeeping process.

## 2. Receipt & Invoice Processing Skill

Manually entering information from receipts and invoices can take hours, especially when you're handling hundreds of transactions.

The **Receipt & Invoice Processing Skill** helps extract important financial information from these documents.

For example, you could upload a folder of receipts and invoices, and Claude could help identify the vendor, transaction date, amount, tax, and other relevant details.

The extracted information can then be organized into a structured format for review or import into an accounting system.

**What this skill can do:**

- Extract information from receipts and invoices.
- Identify vendors, dates, amounts, and taxes.
- Organize transaction data into spreadsheets.
- Flag missing or unclear information.
- Prepare extracted records for bookkeeping.

**Best for:** Bookkeepers and accountants who regularly process large numbers of receipts and invoices.

## 3. Expense Categorization Skill

Recording an expense is one thing. Recording it under the correct accounting category is another.

Businesses spend money on software, travel, equipment, advertising, utilities, and many other things.

The **Expense Categorization Skill** helps classify these transactions using a consistent accounting structure.

For example, you could provide a list of transactions from a bank statement.

Claude can help identify the likely expense categories based on the transaction descriptions, your chart of accounts, and the business context.

**What this skill can do:**

- Categorize business expenses.
- Match transactions to a chart of accounts.
- Identify potentially misclassified transactions.
- Flag expenses that need additional information.
- Prepare categorized transaction records for review.

**Best for:** Bookkeepers managing daily transactions and accountants reviewing expense records.

## 4. Bank Reconciliation Skill

Bank reconciliation is one of the most important bookkeeping tasks.

You need to make sure the transactions recorded in your accounting system match what actually happened in the bank account.

But when you're dealing with hundreds or thousands of transactions, finding discrepancies can be frustrating.

The **Bank Reconciliation Skill** helps compare bank statements with accounting records and identify differences.

For example, you could provide a bank statement and a general ledger export for the same period.

Claude can help match transactions, identify unmatched entries, and prepare a reconciliation report.

**What this skill can do:**

- Match bank transactions with accounting records.
- Identify missing or duplicate transactions.
- Flag unmatched deposits and withdrawals.
- Calculate reconciliation differences.
- Prepare bank reconciliation reports.

**Best for:** Bookkeepers and accountants responsible for keeping bank balances accurate.

## 5. Monthly Bookkeeping Close Skill

At the end of every month, accountants and bookkeepers need to make sure the financial records are complete and accurate.

This usually involves reconciling accounts, checking transactions, reviewing balances, and preparing reports.

The **Monthly Bookkeeping Close Skill** helps organize this process.

For example, you could use it to prepare a month-end checklist for a business and review the records that need attention before closing the books.

**What this skill can do:**

- Create month-end closing checklists.
- Review outstanding bookkeeping tasks.
- Identify unreconciled accounts.
- Organize adjusting entries for review.
- Prepare month-end financial reporting packages.

**Best for:** Bookkeepers and accountants managing monthly financial close processes for businesses or clients.

## 6. Financial Statement Extraction Skill

Many businesses still receive financial statements as PDFs.

Unfortunately, getting the information from those documents into a spreadsheet can be time-consuming.

The **Financial Statement Extraction Skill** helps convert financial statement data into structured records that can be analyzed or reconciled.

For example, you could upload a PDF bank statement and ask Claude to extract the transactions into a CSV or Excel file.

The skill can also perform checks to help identify missing transactions or differences in totals.

**What this skill can do:**

- Extract transaction data from PDF bank statements.
- Convert financial information into CSV or Excel formats.
- Organize dates, descriptions, debits, and credits.
- Check extracted totals against statement balances.
- Flag possible extraction errors.

**Best for:** Accountants and bookkeepers who frequently work with PDF bank statements and financial records.

## 7. Financial Statement Audit Skill

Even when financial statements have already been prepared, they still need to be reviewed.

A balance sheet might contain unusual balances. An income statement might show unexpected changes. Or two reports might contain figures that don't agree.

The **Financial Statement Audit Skill** helps perform structured checks on financial reports.

For example, you could provide a company's balance sheet and income statement for two periods.

Claude can help identify unusual movements, inconsistencies, and areas that need further investigation.

**What this skill can do:**

- Review balance sheets and income statements.
- Identify unusual account balances.
- Compare financial figures across periods.
- Check basic accounting relationships.
- Prepare lists of potential issues for further review.

This is a financial review assistant, not a replacement for a formal audit conducted by a qualified auditor.

**Best for:** Accountants reviewing financial records and bookkeepers performing quality checks.

## 8. Financial Analysis & Ratios Skill

Financial reports contain a lot of numbers, but those numbers don't always explain how a business is performing.

The **Financial Analysis & Ratios Skill** helps turn accounting data into useful financial insights.

For example, you could provide a company's income statement and balance sheet.

Claude can calculate relevant financial ratios and explain what they suggest about profitability, liquidity, and financial performance.

**What this skill can do:**

- Calculate profitability ratios.
- Analyze liquidity and working capital.
- Review revenue and expense trends.
- Compare financial performance across periods.
- Prepare financial analysis reports.

**Best for:** Accountants, financial analysts, and business owners who want to understand financial performance.

## 9. Accrual Accounting & Adjustments Skill

Not every financial transaction should be recorded only when money enters or leaves a bank account.

Under accrual accounting, revenue and expenses are generally recognized when earned or incurred, even if payment happens later.

The **Accrual Accounting & Adjustments Skill** helps organize the calculations and entries involved in this process.

For example, you could provide unpaid invoices, outstanding expenses, and other financial records at the end of a reporting period.

Claude can help identify potential accruals and prepare proposed adjusting entries for review.

**What this skill can do:**

- Identify potential accrued expenses and revenue.
- Support cash-to-accrual accounting adjustments.
- Prepare proposed adjusting journal entries.
- Organize supporting calculations and working papers.
- Identify transactions that may belong to different accounting periods.

**Best for:** Accountants preparing accrual-based financial records and period-end adjustments.

## 10. Financial Statements Preparation Skill

Preparing financial statements requires more than simply adding up income and expenses.

The underlying accounting records need to be organized correctly, and the resulting reports need to agree with each other.

The **Financial Statements Preparation Skill** helps turn accounting records into structured financial reports.

For example, you could provide a trial balance and supporting accounting schedules.

Claude can help organize the figures into financial statements and perform basic consistency checks.

**What this skill can do:**

- Prepare income statements.
- Organize balance sheet information.
- Support cash flow statement preparation.
- Check totals and accounting relationships.
- Prepare financial reports for review.

**Best for:** Accountants and bookkeepers preparing periodic financial statements for businesses and clients.

## 11. Tax Preparation Skill

Tax preparation becomes much easier when financial records are properly organized.

But gathering transactions, identifying deductible expenses, and preparing supporting schedules can still take a lot of time.

The **Tax Preparation Skill** helps organize accounting information for tax reporting.

For example, you could provide a business's categorized expenses and income records for the year.

Claude can help prepare summaries and supporting schedules that make it easier to review the information needed for tax preparation.

**What this skill can do:**

- Organize income and expense records for tax preparation.
- Prepare tax-related financial summaries.
- Identify transactions that may need additional documentation.
- Create supporting tax schedules.
- Flag missing information for review.

Tax rules vary by country, and the source workflow is primarily designed around U.S. requirements. Final tax calculations and filings should be reviewed against current applicable laws.

**Best for:** Accountants, tax preparers, and bookkeepers preparing business records for tax season.

## 12. Fixed Assets & Depreciation Skill

When a business purchases equipment, vehicles, computers, or other long-term assets, the accounting treatment can be different from an ordinary expense.

These assets may need to be tracked and depreciated over time.

The **Fixed Assets & Depreciation Skill** helps manage the related records and calculations.

For example, you could provide a list of assets with their purchase dates, costs, and expected useful lives.

Claude can help organize an asset register and calculate depreciation schedules using the method you specify.

**What this skill can do:**

- Create fixed asset registers.
- Organize asset purchase details.
- Calculate depreciation schedules.
- Track accumulated depreciation and book values.
- Prepare proposed depreciation journal entries.

**Best for:** Accountants and bookkeepers managing businesses with equipment, vehicles, and other fixed assets.

## 13. Contractor Payment & 1099 Tracking Skill

Businesses that work with independent contractors often need to track payments separately from employee payroll.

In the United States, certain contractor payments may also need to be reported using Form 1099.

The **Contractor Payment & 1099 Tracking Skill** helps organize these records throughout the year.

For example, you could provide a list of contractor payments and supporting information.

Claude can help organize the payment totals and identify records that may need additional review before tax reporting.

**What this skill can do:**

- Track contractor payments.
- Organize contractor records.
- Calculate payment totals by contractor.
- Identify missing tax documentation.
- Prepare 1099 reporting summaries for review.

**Best for:** U.S. accountants, bookkeepers, and businesses that regularly pay independent contractors.

## 14. Estimated Tax Calculation Skill

Some businesses and self-employed individuals need to make estimated tax payments during the year instead of waiting until they file their annual returns.

Calculating these payments can be difficult when income changes from month to month.

The **Estimated Tax Calculation Skill** helps organize the relevant figures and estimate potential payment requirements.

For example, you could provide a business's year-to-date income, expenses, and previous tax payments.

Claude can help calculate estimated taxable income and prepare payment scenarios using the applicable rules and assumptions.

**What this skill can do:**

- Estimate business taxable income.
- Organize quarterly tax calculations.
- Track estimated tax payments.
- Compare projected tax obligations with payments already made.
- Flag possible payment shortfalls for review.

The underlying skill is focused on U.S. estimated tax requirements. Calculations should be verified using current tax rules before making payments.

**Best for:** U.S. tax professionals, accountants, and bookkeepers working with self-employed individuals or businesses that make estimated tax payments.

## How to Use the Accounting & Bookkeeping Skills Bundle

The Accounting & Bookkeeping Skills Bundle is organized so you can find and install the skills that are useful for your work without setting up everything.

Here's how to get started:

1. **Visit the GitHub repository.** Open the Accounting & Bookkeeping Skills Bundle and browse the available skills.
2. **Choose the skills you need.** Each skill focuses on a specific accounting or bookkeeping task, so you don't have to install all 14.
3. **Check the requirements.** Some skills work with financial documents and spreadsheets, while others may require accounting software integrations, API keys, or additional setup.
4. **Install your chosen skills.** Follow the installation instructions provided in the repository for your Claude environment.
5. **Start using them.** Open Claude, provide the relevant financial records, and ask it to use the appropriate skill.

For example, you could install the Bank Reconciliation Skill to help match bank statements with accounting records, or the Monthly Bookkeeping Close Skill to organize your month-end accounting process.

You can start with just one or two skills and add more as needed.

Because accounting involves sensitive financial information, make sure you have permission to process the records you upload. Always review important calculations, financial reports, and tax-related outputs before relying on them.

The goal of the bundle is simple: **give accountants, bookkeepers, and small business owners one place to find practical Claude Skills that help them save time, reduce repetitive work, and manage financial records more efficiently.**
